# What progress can the app honestly show the learner?

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 05

## Question

Decide what the app records and presents as progress for an independent learner. Distinguish first-move success from completing a line, independent from assisted attempts, practice from retention, and retention from unmeasured transfer into games. Which history, review controls, difficulty settings, and progress feedback are useful without speed pressure or unsupported mastery claims?

Set learner-visible meanings and persistence expectations, including whether history must survive reloads and whether old learning records carry over. Leave physical storage and migration implementation to the later technical handoff.
