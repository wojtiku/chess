# What content belongs in the first complete learning release?

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 05, 09

## Question

Choose the initial concepts, prerequisite assumptions, exercise types, and sufficient variation per concept to make the agreed on-demand learning loop useful. Which of board awareness, tactics, mates, defense, calculation, endgames, strategy, and openings are included now, and which are beyond this release? What prevents repeated positions or a tiny collection from masquerading as a complete curriculum?

Use the sourcing/publication standard and selection policy to scope a feasible original curriculum. Do not adopt the reports' inventories wholesale or reproduce a proprietary curriculum. Record the required coverage and quality targets, not production exercises. Surface research or prototype questions where feasibility or learner reaction still blocks a decision.
