# How should recommendations balance level, concepts, and revisiting?

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02, 04

## Question

Define the initial selection and review policy: what determines a suitable next exercise, how learner-selected topics interact with recommendations, how difficulty changes, and when failed or previously solved exercises return. Decide whether related-position teaching, mixed practice, and spaced review belong in the initial release and what evidence routes the learner between them.

Choose a comprehensible product policy and fallback behavior for a new learner, sparse content, repeated failure, and an empty review queue. Do not invent precise mastery estimates or optimize a statistical model before identifying observable signals.
